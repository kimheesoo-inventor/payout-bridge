Payout Bridge

See why a Shopify Payments bank payout is different from your order totals, using two CSV exports.

Runs only in this browser tab. Your files are read on this device and are not uploaded anywhere. Once the page has loaded, it makes no network calls, has no analytics and loads no outside scripts or fonts.

1. Choose your two CSV files

Shopify admin: Finance > Payouts, open one payout (or View transactions), then Export. Shopify emails the CSV to you. One payout at a time is easiest to check against one bank deposit.

Shopify admin: Orders > Export (choose CSV).

The sample files are made up for this demo. They do not come from any real store.

Limits of this tool

  • This is not accounting or tax advice. Check results against your own records.
  • It does not connect to Shopify or to a bank. It only reads the two files you choose.
  • Column names may differ by store, language or export version, so check the mapping in step 2.
  • Version 0 does not handle multiple currencies, or other payment providers such as PayPal or Amazon Pay.
  • Amounts must use a dot for decimals (for example 1,234.50). Formats like 1.234,50 are not read.
  • Orders are matched by order name (for example #1001). Payout lines without an order (such as adjustments) are counted in the payout totals but not matched.
  • Reserves, held funds and payouts split across currencies are not modelled.
  • If the payout file has no Payout ID column, lines are grouped by payout date. One payout date can include more than one payout, so one group may match more than one bank deposit.
